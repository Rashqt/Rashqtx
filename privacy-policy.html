Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

This is a personal website. I'm not a company, and I don't have a legal team, but I still want to be clear about what happens with your information when you use this site.

What this site collects

Most of this site works without collecting anything about you at all. A few specific features handle data differently, described below.

Newsletter

If you enter your email address into the newsletter form, that email is collected so I can send you updates from this site. I don't sell or share it with anyone else.

Local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, to remember a few small things: your light or dark theme preference, and which blog posts you've liked. This data stays on your own device. It isn't sent to me, and I can't see it.

Contact and email

The "Say hi" and email links on this site simply open your own email app. I only see what you choose to send me directly.

Support Rash / UPI payments

The Support Rash feature builds a standard UPI payment link and QR code using the amount you choose. If you use it, the payment happens directly between you and your own UPI app or bank. This site does not process, store, or have access to any payment or banking information.

Third-party links

This site links out to places like YouTube, Instagram, GitHub, Discord, and Amazon. Once you click through, you're on their site, governed by their own privacy policy, not mine.

Fonts and libraries

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a small QR code library from a public CDN. Loading these may involve a request to those services, similar to visiting any website that uses external fonts or scripts.

Analytics

This site does not currently use any analytics or tracking software.

Changes to this policy

If anything about how this site works changes, I'll update this page to match.

Questions

Reach out at workforrash@gmail.com if you have any questions about this policy.
